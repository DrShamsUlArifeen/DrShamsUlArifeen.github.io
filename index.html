About

Rigorous computation,
useful understanding.

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Mathematics at the National University of Sciences & Technology, Islamabad. My work connects numerical analysis, approximation theory, and modern computational methods.

I completed my PhD in Applied Mathematics at Ghulam Ishaq Khan Institute of Engineering Sciences and Technology, where my thesis developed Galerkin and Petrov–Galerkin finite element methods for integer- and fractional-order differential equations.
